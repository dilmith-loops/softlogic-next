Your 3-Minute Breast Self-Exam

Follow along. Six steps, about 30 seconds each.

Step 1 of 6

Look in the mirror

Stand straight, arms at your sides, and look for any visible change.

Step 2 of 6

Raise your arms

Lift your arms high and look again from a new angle.

Step 3 of 6

Check the nipples

Look closely at each nipple for discharge or skin changes.

Step 4 of 6

Lie down and feel

Use the pads of three fingers in small, coin-sized circles.

Step 5 of 6

Follow a pattern

Move up and down in strips so you don't miss any area.

Step 6 of 6

Know when to see a doctor

Most lumps aren't cancer, but every new change deserves a check.

Check complete

Nice work. Repeat this once a month, and see a doctor if you notice anything new or different.
